[ecoplus_project.html](ecoplus_project.html).

| Company | What it does | Rewards |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Big Scrappers | Courier pickup from an app | Cash or points |

| Go Clean | Factories and cafes first, then household pickup by phone or WhatsApp | Not found |
| RecycoLife | Social media ads, workers buy valuable waste | Pays for material |
| Dawarha | Reverse vending machines with AI | App rewards |

## Where EcoPlus can differ

- Verified weight, QR and GPS on every shipment, and a direct link to factory orders and invoices (traceability).
- AI check of the whole bag before pickup (Phase 4); no competitor source mentions it.
- Aim first at businesses with large loads, where the feasibility model is strongest.

## Risks

- Bekia is well funded; competing on household pickup alone is unlikely to work.
- Informal collectors (Zabaleen) may resist; plan to work with them.
- Next research: try the Bekia and Plstka apps, call 2 or 3 of their business customers, check prices per kg.
