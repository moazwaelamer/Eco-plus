# Design brief

Status: written brief only. No visual designs, colours or fonts are decided yet (Linear MOA-210 and MOA-211 are open).

## Principles

- The main action is fast: photograph a bag, see "ready / wait", get a code. At most three taps.
- Drivers and employees work with gloves, outdoors, one-handed: large buttons, high contrast, big numbers for codes and weights.
- Arabic first with full right-to-left support, English second. Numbers can be Western digits.
- Weak connections are normal: show clear states for loading, failed and retry; never lose a typed weight.
- Colour carries meaning (green = collected / earned, amber = waiting, red = rejected) but never alone: always an icon or label.
- Points feel like a loyalty card: a clear level bar and the next goal, not a game full of noise.

## Inspiration to review

Bekia and Plstka apps (competitors), loyalty-card apps, delivery driver apps (stop list + map button).

## To decide with the designer

Brand name and logo, colour palette and fonts (design tokens), illustration style, dark mode, Figma file structure.
